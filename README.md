A Multi-device Collaborative Digital Archive of Yellow River Culture

· Tech Stack: Built with OpenHarmony and ArkTS, supporting multi-device synchronized compilation and both offline/online modes.
· Immersive Experience: Features built-in 3D/VR exhibition halls and AR holographic projections of the Northern Song Dynasty's Dongjing City.
· Intelligent Guide: Integrated with AI assistant "Xiao Nü Qingluan" (Little Green Phoenix) to provide instant insights into Yellow River culture.
· Exclusive Services: Supports multi-dimensional searching by location, category, and dynasty, smart LBS recommendations for nearby artifacts, and provides collection and note-taking features.
